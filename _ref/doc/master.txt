[Heading1] TESSERACT — BRAND IDENTITY &amp; LOGO DIRECTION
[Heading2] Brand Name
TESSERACT
[Heading2] Tagline
Build beyond the ordinary.
[Heading2] What the Name Represents
Tesseract is a geometric concept associated with a fourth dimension. For the agency, the name represents thinking beyond the obvious, exploring new dimensions of digital experiences, and building work that goes beyond ordinary websites.
[Heading2] Logo Direction
The logo should feel intelligent, minimal, technical and timeless. It should avoid looking like a generic technology company or an overly complicated futuristic brand.
[Heading2] Core Logo Elements
[ListBullet] A geometric symbol inspired by the structure or dimensional perspective of a tesseract.
[ListBullet] A clean TESSERACT wordmark using strong, modern typography.
[ListBullet] A standalone symbol that works as a favicon, app icon, social avatar and small-scale brand mark.
[ListBullet] Simple geometric construction that gives the identity a precise, engineered character.
[ListBullet] A flexible monochrome version for premium applications, with an optional digital accent colour for interactive experiences.
[Heading2] Visual Personality
Minimal • Intelligent • Precise • Experimental • Premium • Digital • Human
[Heading2] Brand Idea
We don't simply make websites look different. We think differently about how a digital experience can work, feel and communicate.
[Heading2] Primary Brand Lockup
TESSERACTBuild beyond the ordinary.
Use the full lockup in key brand moments. The TESSERACT wordmark or geometric symbol can be used independently across the website, social media, favicon and other digital applications.
